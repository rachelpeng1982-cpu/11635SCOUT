## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for the 2027 Hsinchu Scout Day event ("Brave Winds of Hsinchu: Field Expedition Island") held at Dongmen Elementary School. The visual identity evokes an authentic, disciplined outdoor exploration experience, rejecting cartoonish visuals and synthetic neon aesthetics.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Disciplined & Honorable:** Reflects the rigor, oath, and timeless values of the scouting tradition.
- **Tactile Expedition:** Infuses the tactile character of weathered parchment task manuals, stitched canvas badge patches, brass compasses, and oiled saddle leather.
- **Natural Heritage:** Rooted in the native pine and moss landscapes of northern Taiwan, conveying grounded confidence and environmental stewardship.

### Aesthetic Execution
- **Design Movement:** Tactile Field Manual / Modern Heritage Minimal.
- **Visual Weight:** Solid, grounded components with refined parchment background tiers, stitched or debossed perimeter micro-borders, and tactile hardware finishes (antique brass, leatherette tags).
- **Physical Metaphor:** Interface elements behave like archival field equipment and pocket field guides—durable, purpose-driven, and clearly legible under bright outdoor sunlight.

## Colors

The palette is derived from authentic field survival elements: weathered expedition paper, highland conifer forests, brass navigation tools, and sturdy leather tack.

### Core Palette Allocation
- **Primary — Deep Pine Green (`#1B382B` / deep shade `#14281F`):** Used for authoritative headers, structural containers, primary interactive states, and deep canvas framing.
- **Secondary — Compass Bronze Gold (`#D4A359` / aged `#B88235`):** Serves as an accent of honor and achievement. Applied to scout badges, checkpoint indicators, star ratings, and subtle highlight trims.
- **Tertiary — Camp Leather Brown (`#5A3E2B` / warm strap `#7D573C`):** Used for auxiliary badges, category chips, section dividers, and tactile borders that anchor content cards.
- **Accent Support — Moss Canopy Green (`#3D6647` / vibrant `#5C8C67`):** Marks completion states, active filters, and progression rings.
- **Functional Alert — Terracotta Hazard Red (`#A8382B`):** Reserved strictly for critical field alerts, timer count-downs, and emergency outpost contacts.
- **Neutral Canvas — Field Parchment (`#F7F4EA` / base tone `#EFE9D8`):** The primary surface color, providing an eye-resting, paper-like ground that prevents the glare of pure white during daytime outdoor operations.

## Typography

Noto Sans (paired with Noto Sans TC in localized rendering) provides crisp, neutral clarity. Type hierarchy relies on deliberate weight jumps rather than decorative typefaces, reinforcing the look of a practical field logbook.

### Typesetting Rules
- **High Readability Outdoors:** Headings and mission titles utilize bold weights (`700`) paired with Deep Pine Green (`#14281F`) for contrast against light parchment backgrounds.
- **Letter Spacing:** Labels and metadata tags incorporate positive letter spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`), referencing stamped military gear and engraved survey tools.
- **Line Heights:** Generous line heights across body text (`1.5` ratio) prevent visual fatigue during prolonged reading under variable outdoor lighting conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive fluid container model optimized for handheld mobile use during outdoor orienteering, while remaining structured and readable on tablet checkpoint stations.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) lateral canvas margins and 16px gutters. Elements emphasize vertical thumb reachability with compact vertical stacks.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. Suitable for patrol leader dashboards and map split-views.
- **Desktop / Station Kiosk (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed out at 1200px width with 40px (`2.5rem`) lateral margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Spacing operates on a strict 4px/8px modular base.
- Mission blocks, map sections, and checklist items are separated by `space-lg` (24px) to avoid accidental taps when operating devices with cold or gloved hands.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material cues rather than synthetic neon or excessive multi-layered dropshadows. Surfaces simulate pressed parchment cards resting over dense, dark leather and canvas surfaces.

### Depth Techniques
- **Parchment Layering:** Deep canvas base (`#EFE9D8`) holds upper cards (`#F7F4EA`), creating immediate distinction via soft tonal variance.
- **Stitched Perimeter Micro-Borders:** Content cards utilize a subtle, warm border (`1px solid rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.16)` or `rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.12)`) simulating the edge of heavy pressed paper or a fabric path patch.
- **Tactile Ambient Shadow:** Where elevation is necessary (e.g., active task modal, floating navigation bar), shadows use low-opacity, warm earth tones:
  - Resting elevation: `0 2px 4px rgba(27, 40, 31, 0.08)`
  - Raised badge/card: `0 6px 14px -2px rgba(27, 40, 31, 0.12), 0 2px 6px rgba(90, 62, 43, 0.06)`
  - Active pressed state: `0 1px 2px rgba(27, 40, 31, 0.16)` with a `1px` downward translation.

## Shapes

The design uses a defined 20px curvature for cards and structural containers, striking a balance between the precision of military map coordinate cards and the soft corners of pressed leather field pouches.

### Corner Radius System
- **Main Structural Cards & Modals:** Standardized to `20px` (`rounded-xl` equivalent).
- **Interactive Buttons & Badge Chips:** `20px` to maintain a pill-like, tactile token silhouette reminiscent of stitched scout achievement patches.
- **Inner Micro Elements (Inputs, Checkboxes, Icons):** `8px` (`rounded-sm`) to retain crisp internal structure without conflicting with the exterior 20px envelope.

## Components

### Buttons (Achievement Badge Metaphor)
- **Minimum Hit Target:** All interactive controls maintain a strict minimum height of `48px` (exceeding the 44px ergonomic threshold) for reliable operation during rapid outdoor movement.
- **Primary Button:** Deep Pine Green background (`#1B382B`), parchment text (`#F7F4EA`), 20px radius, bounded by an interior `1px` inset ring (`rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.4)`) referencing bronze metal eyelets.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Compass Bronze Gold background (`#D4A359`), dark pine text (`#14281F`), bold weight, with subtle `0 2px 0 #B88235` physical keypress baseline.
- **Ghost / Outlined Button:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` Camp Leather Brown border (`#5A3E2B`), text in `#5A3E2B`.

### Task & Mission Cards
- **Construction:** Paper-textured parchment background (`#F7F4EA`) enclosed with a delicate `1px` border (`#D8D0BA`). 20px corner radius.
- **Header Strip:** Features a small bronze or leather-brown status ribbon indicator (e.g., "關卡 04 — 東門城樓解密").
- **State Indicators:** Incomplete tasks feature an unfilled brass compass ring icon; completed missions show an embroidered moss-green badge graphic with an inset check.

### Chips & Station Badges
- **Form:** 20px pill shapes with a standard height of 32px.
- **Colors:** Category tags use subdued tones—Forest Ecology (`#3D6647` fill, `#F7F4EA` text), Pioneer Knotting (`#5A3E2B` fill, `#F7F4EA` text), Orienteering (`#B88235` fill, `#14281F` text).

### Form Inputs & Checkpoints
- **Input Fields:** Minimum height 48px, parchment ground (`#FFFFFF` with subtle `#F7F4EA` tone), `1.5px` border in `#C5BCA7`, 12px radius. Focused state shifts the border to Deep Pine Green (`#1B382B`) with a soft `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.15)` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Heavy-duty square/circle tokens (22px dimension) using `#1B382B` active fills and `#D4A359` check icons, avoiding standard browser styling.

### Map & Coordinate HUD Overlays
- **Field Navigation Floater:** Fixed bottom quick-action panel styled like an expedition utility pouch: Deep Pine Green surface, 20px radius, bronze divider lines, providing one-touch access to "My Patrol", "Field Map", and "QR Code Checkpoint Scanner".
